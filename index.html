Senior Design Project (EE/CPRE 4910/4920)

  • Project Title: Compact 24–40 GHz MIMO FMCW Radar
  • Description: Design and construction of a compact, portable four-antenna MIMO radar operating from 24–40 GHz with dual circular polarization. The system is intended for applications including ground-surface mapping through vegetation and radar vision through degraded visual environments (DVE), such as fog and dust, for detection of drones, personnel, and vehicles.
  • Your Role: Contributing to the design, development, and integration of the radar system, with work focusing primarily on high-speed sampling and signal processing.
  • Skills & Knowledge Gained: RF and high-frequency circuit design, PCB design, signal processing, and numerical electromagnetic modeling for antenna design.
  • Big Picture Contribution: Development of a compact radar platform capable of sensing and imaging in environments where conventional optical systems can be degraded by vegetation, fog, dust, and other visibility limitations.

Project 3: Microwave Amplifier Design

  • Skills Learned: Microwave amplifier design, S-parameter analysis, impedance matching, unconditional stability analysis, microstrip transmission lines, and RF PCB design.

  • Design Requirements: Gain, input/output matching, 5% fractional bandwidth, and unconditional stability from 10 MHz to 6 GHz.
