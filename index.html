Project 2: 5-Element Yagi-Uda Antenna Design & CST Simulation

  • Description: Electromagnetic design, element thickness compensation, and CST simulation of a 5-element Yagi-Uda antenna array (1 reflector, 1 driven element, and 3 directors) optimized for a resonant frequency of 50.1 MHz (λ = 5.988 m).
  • Your Role:
    • Modeled array geometry in CST Studio Suite incorporating parasitic element (d/λ = 0.00424) and boom diameter (D/λ = 0.00852) thickness compensation.
    • Designed and modeled a folded dipole driving element to overcome impedance matching challenges and provide a higher input impedance for direct matching to a 50Ω feeding port.
  • Skills Learned:
    • CST Studio Suite & MATLAB simulation
    • Yagi-Uda array synthesis & analytical formulations
    • Folded dipole feeder design
    • S11 port impedance matching
    • 3D radiation pattern analysis & directivity optimization
    • Front-to-back ratio tuning & side-lobe level reduction
  • Resources Used: CST Studio Suite, Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design (Balanis), MATLAB, and analytical array formulations.
  • Analysis & Results:
    • Achieved a simulated directivity of ~10.6 dBi in CST.
    • Achieved an S11 return loss of -20 dB at 50.1 MHz, indicating excellent power transfer and input port matching.
    • The folded dipole provided a highly effective match to the 50Ω feed line, yielding a high front-to-back ratio and low side-lobe levels.

Project 3: Microwave Amplifier Design
