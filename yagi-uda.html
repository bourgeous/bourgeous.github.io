5-Element Yagi-Uda Antenna Design

A 5-element Yagi-Uda antenna designed and simulated for operation at 50.1 MHz using CST Studio Suite. The array consists of a reflector, driven element, and three directors optimized for directional radiation and impedance matching.

The design incorporates element-diameter compensation, along with a folded-dipole driven element for impedance transformation and direct matching to a 50 Ω port.

02. Element Optimization

The element dimensions and spacing were optimized around the 50.1 MHz design frequency. Element diameter effects were compensated using normalized geometric dimensions.

  • Parameters:
    • L1 - reflector length
    • L2 - folded dipole total length
    • A - length of straight portion of folded dipole
    • l2gap - feeding gap in folded dipole
    • R - radius of bent ends of folded dipole
    • L3 - first director length
    • L4 - second director length
    • L5 - third director length
    • diameter, diameter2 - element thickness

    Results

    • 5-element Yagi-Uda array operating at 50.1 MHz.
    • Approximately 10.6 dBi simulated directivity.
    • Approximately −20 dB S11 at the design frequency.
    • Folded-dipole driven element providing impedance transformation to a 50 Ω port.
